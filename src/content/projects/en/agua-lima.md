## Data and method

I built an index for the 43 districts from INEI 2017 Census microdata and geographic data from GADM and OpenStreetMap, processed in PostGIS inside Docker. The maps are generated by script in QGIS.

## Results

- Risk concentrates in a continuous northern and eastern corridor led by San Juan de Lurigancho, Villa María del Triunfo and Puente Piedra.
- Miraflores, San Isidro and San Borja form a cluster of near-universal coverage.
- Moran's I (0.266, p = 0.002) and LISA clusters confirm the pattern is not random, which points to priority areas for intervention.

## Robustness

The ranking stays stable across weighting schemes: the Spearman correlation between them is above 0.95.
